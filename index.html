Prologue

The Year
2198

The year was 2198.

Mumbai no longer looked like the city people remembered from centuries ago. Towering structures of glass and metal disappeared into the clouds. Silent transport systems crossed the sky. Artificial intelligence controlled entire districts. Machines could repair damaged organs, predict storms, and build structures in hours.

Humanity had reached a level of technology that once seemed impossible.

But there was one thing technology had never been able to answer.

"Why did the oldest stories refuse to disappear?"

Ancient temples still stood between futuristic towers. Old weapons were displayed in museums beside machines capable of destroying cities.

And somewhere beneath the modern world, forgotten records spoke of something called:

THE DIVINE CODE.

Most people believed it was a myth.

Professor Ishaan Rao did not.

And one night, while examining an ancient digital manuscript, he discovered a sentence that had not appeared in any historical database.

"When two warriors awaken, the world will mistake the enemy for the evil."

Professor Ishaan stared at the screen.

Then another line appeared.

2198.

His face changed.

"Impossible..."

The lights in his laboratory suddenly went out.

For three seconds, there was complete darkness.

Then a symbol appeared on the screen.

A black circle.
Inside it was a burning red eye.

Professor Ishaan whispered one name.

"Kali Rakshas."

The screen went black.

And somewhere, thousands of kilometres away...

someone opened his eyes.

Chapter 01

The First Sign

Mumbai, 2198.

Aarav Sharma stood on the roof of a futuristic training centre, staring at the city.

Drones moved between buildings. Holographic advertisements floated above the streets.

But Aarav wasn't looking at any of them.

He was holding an ancient metal object.

It had been found beneath an abandoned structure outside Mumbai.

There were no wires. No battery. No visible technology. Yet the object was warm. Almost alive.

Aarav turned it over.

A symbol was engraved on its surface.

The same symbol Professor Ishaan had seen.

His friend Veer walked onto the roof.

"You've been staring at that thing for twenty minutes."

Aarav didn't answer.

Veer looked at the object.

"Okay... that's creepy."

"It wasn't glowing before."

Veer laughed nervously.

"You're saying it glows now?"

The object suddenly produced a faint blue light.

Both boys froze.

The light became brighter.

Then Aarav heard something.

"You are not the first."

Aarav dropped the object.

The blue light disappeared.

Veer stared at him.

"What did you hear?"

Aarav looked toward the city.

"I don't know."

Far away, Professor Ishaan suddenly woke from his chair. He knew.

The first warrior had been found.

But before he could reach Aarav, another signal appeared on his system.

LOCATION: JAPAN

Professor Ishaan's expression turned serious.

"There are two..."

He looked at the ancient manuscript.

"...not one."

THE FIRST SIGN WAS ONLY THE BEGINNING.
Chapter 02

The Man From Japan

CHAPTER 2 IMAGE

Replace chapter2.jpg

Japan.

A futuristic city stood beneath a red moon.

Kaito Ren walked alone through an abandoned temple.

Unlike Mumbai, this place had not been rebuilt. The ancient wooden structure remained untouched.

Kaito had spent years searching for something.

Tradition.

Power.

Meaning.

He believed humanity had become too dependent on machines.

Then he found an ancient chamber beneath the temple.

Inside was another object.

Similar to Aarav's.

But this one was black.

Kaito reached toward it.

BOOM.

Every light in the temple exploded.

A voice echoed through the darkness.

"You were chosen."

Kaito looked around.

"Who are you?"

A shadow appeared behind him.

Two red eyes opened.

But Kaito could not see its face.

"You have spent your life trying to restore what the world abandoned."

Kaito clenched his fist.

"Yes."

"Then I can give you the power to do it."

Kaito looked at the object.

"What do you want?"

The shadow smiled.

"Nothing."

"Except obedience."

Kaito hesitated.

Then he touched the object again.

A wave of dark energy exploded through the temple.

Across the ocean, Aarav suddenly collapsed. The ancient object beside him began glowing blue.

Professor Ishaan looked at his monitor.

Two signals.

Blue.

Red.

His hands trembled.

"The awakening has begun."

But he didn't know something.

The red signal wasn't coming from Kaito.

It was coming from something behind Kaito.

THE ENEMY HAD NOT REVEALED HIMSELF YET.
Chapter 03

The Awakening

CHAPTER 3 IMAGE

Replace chapter3.jpg

Aarav woke inside Professor Ishaan's laboratory.

Veer was sitting beside him.

Meera stood near the window.

Professor Ishaan placed the ancient object on the table.

"You need to understand something."

Aarav sat up.

"What?"

"This isn't technology."

Aarav stared at him.

"Then what is it?"

Professor Ishaan opened an ancient archive.

Thousands of symbols appeared.

"Power."

Aarav laughed.

"That's your explanation?"

Professor Ishaan didn't smile.

"Power existed before machines."

The room became silent.

He showed them records of warriors from thousands of years ago. Some were described as having strength beyond normal humans. Others were said to have received blessings from divine forces.

But every record ended the same way.

Power without wisdom creates destruction.

Suddenly the laboratory shook.

The object on the table lifted into the air.

Aarav stood.

Blue light surrounded him.

The entire building's systems failed.

Meera shouted his name.

Aarav tried to control the energy.

But then he heard the voice again.

"Do not fear what you are becoming."

Aarav looked upward.

"Who are you?"

No answer.

Meanwhile, in Japan...

Kaito was experiencing the same awakening.

But his energy was different.

Red.

Dark.

Violent.

And beside him stood the shadow.

Kali Rakshas.

For the first time, Kaito saw its face.

But the creature didn't attack.

It simply said:

"Your enemy has awakened too."

Kaito looked toward India.

"Who?"

Kali Rakshas smiled.

"Aarav Sharma."

Kaito's eyes hardened.

"Then I will stop him."

The creature had won its first battle.

It had created an enemy where there should have been a choice.

THE TWO WARRIORS WERE NOW ON A COLLISION COURSE.
Chapter 04

The Manipulator

CHAPTER 4 IMAGE

Replace chapter4.jpg

Weeks passed.

Aarav trained.

Kaito trained.

Both became stronger.

But something strange happened. Whenever Aarav discovered something about Kaito, the information was wrong. Whenever Kaito heard something about Aarav, it was also wrong.

Someone was feeding them lies.

Kali Rakshas watched from the shadows.

It didn't need to fight them.

It only needed to make them hate each other.

Kaito was told that Aarav wanted to destroy Japanese traditions.

Aarav was told that Kaito wanted to use divine power to control India.

Neither was completely true.

But anger doesn't require truth.

It only requires a reason.

One night, Kaito stood on a mountain.

Kali Rakshas appeared behind him.

"You have been betrayed."

Kaito turned.

"By whom?"

"The one who carries the blue power."

Kaito's fist tightened.

"Why?"

"Because he believes his blessing makes him superior."

Kaito looked toward the horizon.

"Then I'll prove him wrong."

Kali Rakshas smiled.

Exactly what he wanted.

Meanwhile, Aarav received a message.

KAITO IS COMING.

Aarav stood.

"Then I'll be ready."

Meera grabbed his arm.

"Something isn't right."

Aarav looked at her.

"What?"

"Both of you are hearing the same story."

Aarav went silent.

She continued.

"Someone wants you to fight."

Before Aarav could respond, every screen in the city turned black.

A single message appeared:

MIDNIGHT.

Then coordinates.

Aarav stared at them.

Meera whispered:

"Don't go."

Aarav looked at the coordinates.

"I have to."

He didn't know that Kaito had received the exact same message.

And above them...

Kali Rakshas watched.

THE TRAP WAS SET.
Chapter 05

The Divine Conflict

CHAPTER 5 IMAGE

Replace chapter5.jpg

Midnight.

The battlefield was abandoned.

Above it hung an enormous moon.

Aarav arrived first.

Then Kaito appeared.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Aarav looked at him.

"Why are you doing this?"

Kaito answered:

"Because you stand against everything I believe in."

Aarav frowned.

"I never said that."

Kaito's expression changed.

"What?"

Aarav stepped forward.

"Who told you I wanted to destroy your traditions?"

Silence.

Kaito realized something.

The same thought entered both their minds.

Someone had lied to them.

Then they heard laughter.

Kali Rakshas emerged from the darkness.

Kaito turned.

"You..."

The creature smiled.

"You were never meant to understand."

Aarav's blue energy erupted.

Kaito's red energy exploded.

The battlefield shook.

Kali Rakshas disappeared into the darkness.

The two warriors attacked.

Blue collided with red.

The sky flashed.

The ground cracked.

Aarav shouted.

Kaito answered.

Their powers grew stronger with every strike.

Then both unleashed everything.

A gigantic blue sphere formed behind Aarav.

A gigantic red sphere formed behind Kaito.

Energy rings expanded across the battlefield.

The moon disappeared behind the light.

The two forces collided.

BOOOOOOM.

The world seemed to stop.

And then—

silence.

A figure appeared between them.

Neither warrior could move.

The Divine Guardian.

Its voice echoed across the battlefield.

"Enough."

Aarav dropped to one knee.

Kaito stared in disbelief.

The Guardian looked at both warriors.

"You were given power."

It turned toward Kaito.

"But you allowed another to choose your enemy."

Then toward Aarav.

"And you allowed anger to choose yours."

Kali Rakshas emerged from the darkness.

"You cannot stop what has already begun."

The Guardian looked at him.

"I do not need to."

The ancient object in Aarav's hand suddenly cracked. Kaito's object cracked too.

The two symbols combined in the air.

A hidden image appeared.

Two warriors.

Standing together.

Not fighting.

Aarav stared.

"So this was never supposed to be a battle..."

Kaito slowly lowered his hand.

Kali Rakshas screamed.

The Guardian disappeared.

For the first time, Aarav and Kaito looked at each other without hatred.

But before either could speak—

The sky turned black.

A third symbol appeared above them.

Something far more powerful than Kali Rakshas was watching.

Professor Ishaan saw it from thousands of kilometres away.

His face went pale.

"No..."

Meera asked:

"What is it?"

Professor Ishaan whispered:

"The two warriors were never the prophecy."

"They were the warning."
